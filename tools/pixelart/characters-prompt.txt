A sprite sheet of three original fantasy RPG characters in authentic 16-bit era console pixel art, in the style of the overworld walking sprites of early-1990s Japanese role-playing games. True pixel art: hard square pixels, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur, limited palette of about 14 colours per character, dark coloured outlines, three-tone shading lit from the upper left.

Layout: a strict grid of 9 columns and 3 rows on a perfectly flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) background, with nothing else in the image: no text, no labels, no borders, no grid lines, no shadows on the ground. Every sprite is the same size, centred in its cell, drawn as a 16 pixel wide by 24 pixel tall sprite (large head, compact body, classic proportions) and shown enlarged with crisp pixels.

Row 1: a stern young swordsman, tawny brown hair, steel pauldrons over a dark blue tabard, leather belt and boots, a greatsword slung across his back.
Row 2: a sorceress with long dark plum hair and a thin gold circlet, a pale grey-cream robe with a wine-red centre stripe, holding a wooden staff topped with a pale blue crystal.
Row 3: a wary archer with dark skin, short black hair under a red headband, an olive green tunic, leather boots, a longbow and a quiver of arrows.

Columns, the same for every row: columns 1-3 are the character walking toward the viewer (facing front): left foot forward, standing with feet together, right foot forward. Columns 4-6 are the character walking to the right in profile: stride, feet together, opposite stride. Columns 7-9 are the character walking away from the viewer (seen from behind): left foot forward, feet together, right foot forward.

Each character must look identical in colours, costume and proportions across all nine of its cells. Muted, slightly desaturated, serious palette, not cute, not chibi-cartoonish, not glossy.
